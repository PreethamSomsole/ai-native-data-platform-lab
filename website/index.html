01 / THE PLATFORM

Enterprise data has context.
AI needs to be able to read it.

Teams know which revenue definition belongs to Finance, how fresh a table is, and when an answer needs a follow-up question. The platform turns that knowledge into versioned contracts, bounded retrieval, operational signals, and explicit policy.

01
⌗

Define meaning once

Metrics, concepts, entities, ownership, and use cases live in a reviewable semantic registry. Datasets reference shared definitions instead of quietly inventing their own.

02
⌕

Retrieve before reasoning

Deterministic and hybrid discovery narrow a question to a small, scored candidate set. Business fitness, certification, domain, and intended use matter alongside similarity.

03
⟡

Give trust a signal

Runtime context adds freshness, quality, operational health, usage, row counts, and trends to the business contract—so an agent can explain why a source is fit.

A SAFETY FEATURE, NOT AN ERROR

Sometimes the right answer
is a better question.

“What was net revenue last quarter?” can mean Finance recognized revenue or Operations completed-order revenue. Both are valid. The system surfaces the conflict and asks which definition you mean.

04 / PRODUCTION PATH

A reference foundation.
A deliberate path to production.

The design keeps business meaning and action policy explicit. A production deployment adds the identity, scale, governance, and operations controls around those proven boundaries.

01

Identity & access

Authenticate users and workloads; map roles to catalog visibility, domain access, and tool permissions; enforce row and object policy at the data source.

PRODUCTION EXTENSION
02

Reliable context services

Run stateless APIs behind a gateway. Add managed metadata and vector stores, tenant isolation, caching, rate limits, and versioned semantic releases.

DEPLOYMENT ARCHITECTURE
03

Change control

Connect engineering plans to CI, signed artifacts, environment promotion, approvals, and tested rollback. Production writes remain behind dedicated adapters.

HUMAN-APPROVED PROMOTION
04

Operate and improve

Trace each request to retrieved evidence and policy outcome. Measure retrieval quality, abstention, freshness, latency, cost, and incidents with continuous evaluation.

OBSERVABILITY & EVALUATION
↗

Keep the boundary intact. AI proposes and explains. Deterministic services validate, authorize, record, and recover. Connect Snowflake or Databricks behind adapters only after the vendor-neutral contracts are clear.
